# Data sources and attribution

**Retail electricity and gas prices** — [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat)
datasets `nrg_pc_205` (prices for non-household consumers), `nrg_pc_205_c` (the
same prices split into components) and `nrg_pc_203` (gas), retrieved through the
dissemination API in JSON-stat 2.0 format. Reuse permitted with source
acknowledged.

The code in this repository is the author's own work and may be reused freely.
Nothing here is investment advice or a price quotation.
