# Data sources and attribution

**Wholesale electricity prices** — day-ahead auction, CZ bidding zone, retrieved
from the [energy-charts.info](https://energy-charts.info) API, operated by
Fraunhofer Institute for Solar Energy Systems ISE. Licensed
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Underlying market data
originates from ENTSO-E and EPEX SPOT.

**Solar resource** — [PVGIS](https://re.jrc.ec.europa.eu/pvg_tools/en/) v5.3,
SARAH3 database, European Commission Joint Research Centre. Free to use with
attribution.

**Retail price components** — [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat) datasets
`nrg_pc_205`, `nrg_pc_205_c`, `nrg_pc_203`, retrieved through the dissemination
API. Reuse permitted with source acknowledged.
